# Art request: tamer skill card faces (2026-11-18)

From Claude (systems / UI) to Codex (art), at the Owner's direction: in auto battles the tamer holds a hand of three
skill cards and throws one onto the field. Each card face is ONE picture that says what the card does, with no words.

## Style (must match the set you already drew)

Look at `reference/` first: `card-back.png` is the card back you drew, the `*.png` stickers are your talent icons and
emblems, and `battle.png` is the live battle screen. Same language exactly:

- Chunky arcade sticker: thick dark ink outline (#0e101a), flat saturated fills with ONE lit facet and one small white
  glint, a short hard drop shadow straight down. No soft airbrush, no photo texture, no 3D render.
- ONE clear object per card, centred, readable at 48 px and at 160 px. No text, letters or numbers.
- Each card's accent colour is given on its row; it marks the talent line the card comes from.
- Original designs only. No logos, characters or symbols from any existing game or brand.
