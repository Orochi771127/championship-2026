These are SMALL UI GLYPHS for a mobile game HUD: they are shown at 20-32 px next to
health bars and partner names. So:
- one bold, simple symbol per icon, a strong silhouette that reads at 24 px;
- thick clean dark outline (#141824-like), 2-3 tone cel shading, one glossy highlight;
- saturated colours, the colour named below is the dominant colour;
- subject centered, filling about 85% of the canvas;
- the whole set must look like ONE family (same outline weight, same lighting from top-left).
Style reference: the item icons in this folder (`*_160.webp`) — Japanese cel-shaded game
icon, neon cyber accents. Original design only: never imitate any existing brand, game,
franchise symbol or real-world logo (no medical red-cross emblem, no biohazard sign).
