# Art request: tamer roadmap UI emblems (2026-11-18)

From Claude (systems / UI) to Codex (art), at the Owner's direction: new mechanics must look and move like
the game, not read like text. Claude has shipped code-drawn placeholders; this request is for the real art.

## Style (must match the game)

Look at `reference/*.png` first: these are the live arcade HUD.

- Chunky arcade stickers: a thick dark ink outline (#0e101a), flat saturated fills with ONE lit facet and one
  small white glint, a short hard drop shadow straight down. No soft airbrush, no photo texture, no 3D render.
- Reads at 16 px and at 64 px. Simple silhouettes. Centered in the canvas with even padding.
- Palette: gold #ffc531 / #ffe98a / #f08a1c, mint #7af0d6 / #1fb89a, ink #0e101a, white. Purple and cyan
  accents as in the screenshots.
- Original designs only. No logos, no characters or symbols from any existing game or brand.

## Deliverables

Transparent RGBA PNG, 256 x 256 each (they are shown at 16 to 64 px), one file per item, written to `out/`
with exactly these names:

1. `star-on.png`            a lit partner star (five points, rounded joins, gold, lit upper facet, glint)
2. `star-off.png`           the same star as an empty socket pressed into a dark plate
3. `star-burst.png`         the lit star with a short ring of rays and sparks behind it (for the star-up moment)
4. `exp-crystal.png`        a faceted EXP crystal, cyan to mint, the tamer's level currency
5. `tamer-level-badge.png`  a round tamer level badge: a glove or whistle motif on a mint disc, room for a number below
6. `lock-rank.png`          a padlock sticker with a small rank chevron on its body
7. `lock-level.png`         a padlock sticker with a small upward arrow on its body
8. `evolve-ready.png`       an upward double-chevron inside a hexagon, glowing gold: "ready to evolve, needs a level"
9. `card-back.png`          portrait 256 x 360: the back of a tamer skill card, the same sticker language,
                            a hex-grid pattern and a central core emblem (for the skill-card hand that comes next)

Also write `out/NOTES.md`: for each file, one line on what was drawn and anything you want changed in the brief.
