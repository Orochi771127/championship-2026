Create ONE cohesive original game-art concept board for a new independent creature-raising game. This is an early ART_PROPOSAL, not a screenshot, not a spritesheet, not finished runtime art. Middle: a low quadruped creature inspired by a tapir and a river stone, deep teal body, pale mint broad cheeks, four clearly separated broad feet, one smooth coral arch above forehead, a short paddle tail, calm amber eyes; no armor. Right: a small buoyant manta-like creature with a lavender teardrop body, three broad ribbon fins that form a strong readable silhouette, pale warm inner face, dark round eyes, short gold-tipped underside feelers; no clothing and no skull motifs. Middle: an exercise plot with a compact rounded walking/running loop of terracotta stepping slabs, pale sand interior, low teal boundary posts, uncluttered legible ground.
