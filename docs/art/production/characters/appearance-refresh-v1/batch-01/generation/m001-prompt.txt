Create a polished illustrator's character-design comparison board, wide landscape cream paper with two A/B columns. Only text: M001, A, B. Each column has one large broad low gelatinous neutral figure based on Main cell 0 (first sprite), one smaller vertically stretched open-mouth figure based on Main cell 60 (row 8, column 5), one tiny game-scale neutral copy and five swatches. Use source anatomy and exact visible pose relationships as reference, but create fresh HD illustrations, not pixel art or pasted source sprites. Do not add limbs, ears, horns, a tail, a rigid skull or a skeleton.

A: Amber-jelly creature. Translucent honey-yellow body with one broad amber internal core forming a simple arch behind its small warm dark eyes; cream upper-left jelly reflection and a rust-orange mouth. Three stable rounded amber interior islands, all attached to the deforming body surface; they never float independently. The silhouette is a low soft irregular blob, a small source-compatible trailing lobe and ONLY the same detached droplets visible in the corresponding source pose. Keep broad negative space around the eyes so the face is clear at tiny size.

B: Opaque golden custard-gel creature. Smooth satin sunflower-yellow body with a wide cream brow crescent that deforms with the body, two tiny vertical chestnut eyes below the crescent, a broad rust mouth, and two large ochre marbled zones low on the side. This has a visibly simpler, opaque, graphic material and mask-like face, clearly distinct from A's luminous internal amber core. It remains a fully soft blob, not a rock, animal head, furry pet or cup of food. No container, hat or ornament.

In both proposals the smaller figure uses the original Main cell 60's tall narrow stretch, huge open mouth and corresponding droplet positions; the material/face markings stretch WITH that same deforming surface. Do not replace the extreme pose with a newly invented jump. Preserve the source's gold-yellow dominant palette, open-mouth direction and droplet count/placement per depicted cell. Source labels are cell IDs, not verified gameplay names.

Art direction: clean dark ochre ink, large uncluttered shapes, three readable value bands, upper-left soft light, restrained high-definition hand-painted gradients, dark expressive eyes. A/B difference must survive 64 px thumbnail size. Minimal cream paper board, no environment or cast shadow, no extra effects, no long text, logo, signature or watermark. Design proposals only, not approved animation frames.
