# M226 — A/B concept-board prompt

Reference:

Create an illustrator's refined two-option concept board, wide landscape warm cream paper. Only text M226, A, B. Two balanced columns, each with a large mechanical gear-creature pose based on Main cell 0 (first sprite), a smaller extremely flattened edge-on pose based on Main cell 23 (row 3, column 8), a tiny neutral copy, and five palette swatches. Source defines component centers, gear outlines, teeth count, visible overlap, facing and limb-free mechanical topology. Repaint surfaces and face distinctly in fresh HD art. Do not paste source sprites. Do not turn this into a humanoid robot, sphere, cat or unrelated sun mascot.

A: Brushed-steel and enamel gear spirit. Preserve cool gray as the dominant mass and gold-ochre on the existing toothed ring; decorate the front with a broad ivory enamel brow plate, two narrow dark-red glass eye slots and a charcoal lower-face panel. Simple concentric machine engraving with exactly three large visible accent grooves, tiny localized brass fasteners on existing plates only. Side gear components retain the source positions and gray metal. Clear matte metal with one controlled cool highlight; restrained crafted industrial identity.

B: Mineral-inlaid ancient gear spirit. Same source-compatible mechanical body and component centers, but smoked slate-gray stone-like face inlaid flush inside the existing gold-ochre metal toothed ring. Broad angular pale-gray brow plane and one red inset for each original eye, dark charcoal wedge-shaped lower-face inlay; large faceted mineral planes rather than enamel or concentric engraving. Side gears remain metal; mineral inlay is a surface/material design, not new moving anatomy. No floating crystals, cracks opening into new mouths, new teeth or orbiting pieces.

The extreme smaller drawing must be a thin horizontal edge-on gear stack as in source Main cell 23, with little face visible; do not redraw a front-facing gear squashed vertically. Red eyes and gold teeth remain accents, gray remains dominant. No invented gear separation or rotation instructions. Every engraved/inlaid mark is attached to its existing part so it can be redrawn under the original deformation and occlusion.

Clean charcoal ink, three clear value groups, HD hand-painted material shading, fixed upper-left soft lighting, high readability at 64–96 px. Preserve motion topology while making the surface and face identities visibly different. No environment, contact shadow, poster slogan, detailed text, logo, signature, watermark or duplicated extra gears. This is a proposal board, not a packed production atlas or motion acceptance proof.
