# BM07 Cyberspace R1 art direction

BM07 is an independent, opaque, single-layer Battle arena. Verified catalog and reconstruction evidence records no BM00 common dependency, no field object layer, and no animation bundle.

The production replacement carries forward only functional structure: a complete closed digital arena, a high three-quarter tactical view, a quiet central play surface, and six readable standing zones. It does not copy ROM pixels, palette entries, tiles, decoded coordinates, or original geometry.

The original-created palette uses deep navy and indigo for the void and floor, cyan and violet for energy boundaries, and sparse green data glints. The six standing-zone circles are not accepted from the image model. The builder draws them from one fixed radius and a three-column-by-two-row coordinate contract so every border is aligned and reproducible.

This is an internal art-review candidate. It is not gameplay-mounted, owner-approved, runtime-eligible, or shipping-ready.
