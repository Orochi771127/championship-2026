# Championship human-paint environment delta

The Owner approved Candidate C on 2026-09-02 as the mark-making, material and
detail-distribution direction for bounded Battle migration. This approval does
not approve each migrated asset or authorize runtime/shipping promotion.

## Visual system

- **Shape:** large hand-designed masses first; irregular natural contours; structural geometry stays deliberate but not mechanically ornamented.
- **Value:** three primary depth bands; the playfield remains lower contrast than the border and foreground anchors.
- **Color:** local color varies within a narrow family; warm/cool shifts replace blanket saturation and universal gold highlights.
- **Edge:** mix drawn, broken and lost edges. Do not close every contour or sharpen every material boundary.
- **Material:** sand is powdery, rock matte and layered, metal painted/scuffed, glass selectively luminous. Materials must not share one highlight recipe.
- **Light:** one readable source plus soft bounce; no universal rim light.
- **Detail:** broad marks dominate; medium marks describe flow; small accents are rare and focal. Avoid texture sprayed evenly over the canvas.
- **Camera:** preserve verified Battle wide framing and gameplay-readable central space.

## Do

- Leave visible evidence of block-in and brush overlap.
- Let the distance simplify decisively.
- Use asymmetry in natural scenery while preserving gameplay-required arena symmetry.
- Judge at 390×844 contain-fit as well as full resolution.

## Do not

- Use glossy toy-like bevels on every edge.
- Fill empty ground with repeated chips to imply detail.
- Give stone, plants and metal identical sharpness.
- Bake candidate-generated standing circles into production; retain the canonical shared geometry.
