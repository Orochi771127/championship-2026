# BM06 style critique — reducing the generated look

## Overall impression

The current arena is clear and technically usable, but its finish is too uniformly polished. Rock, foliage, sand, masonry, metal and glass all receive similar edge sharpness, highlight frequency and micro-detail. That makes the image feel processed by one material recipe instead of interpreted by an art team.

## Findings

| Finding | Severity | Correction used in the pilot |
|---|---|---|
| Every surface is equally resolved | High | Concentrate drawing around foreground anchors; quiet the central playfield and distance. |
| Repeated small facets cover sand and rock | High | Replace much of the microtexture with broad masses, medium wind strokes and rare sharp accents. |
| Border reads as glossy molded fantasy plastic | High | Use matte painted metal/stone, restrained cyan and selective scuffed edges. |
| Contours are consistently clean and closed | Medium | Introduce lost edges, broken contours, dry-brush overlap and subtle line-weight variation. |
| Symmetry extends beyond gameplay-required geometry | Medium | Preserve the arena/circle structure while varying environmental contours, vegetation and mark clusters. |
| Sky, ground and props share one rendering cadence | Medium | Give sky broad wet shapes, ground directional strokes and structures deliberate drawn edges. |

## What remains valuable

- The wide camera and quiet central playfield are readable.
- The six standing positions and arena silhouette communicate the Battle structure immediately.
- Warm desert neutrals with small cyan accents are a strong family identity.

## Priority recommendation

Candidate C is the preferred target. It retains B's animation-background drawing language while removing enough repeated ground texture to create intentional visual hierarchy. Approval applies to mark-making and material language only; production replacement must continue to use the canonical BM00 geometry layer rather than generated circle coordinates.
