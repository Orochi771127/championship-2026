# Battle light-fence pilot R1

Owner direction: remove the invented ornate stone ring and replace it with a lightweight sports-style metal fence.

The fence is deliberately low, thin, discontinuous, and secondary to creatures. It uses flat stepped values, irregular broken highlights, no gems, no masonry, no arches, and no glossy 3D bevels. Six standing markers retain the current Championship 2026 presentation contract; their coordinates are not represented as ROM-authored geometry.

The environment plates are original-created. ROM pixels, palettes, tiles, cells, and layouts are research-only and do not enter this batch. The original aggregate color measurements are used only by the composite-integrity gate.

This is a BM08/BM10 internal review pilot. It does not replace runtime art and is not shipping-ready.
