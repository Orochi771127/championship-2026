# Generation record

The two 1536 x 1024 source masters were generated through the Codex imagegen
skill as original game-art scenes. Existing original-created Championship
arena art was used only as the editable source to keep the family rendering
language cohesive; no ROM image was supplied to the generator.

BM03 requested a dark volcanic Battle arena with a broad readable basalt
combat surface, lava restricted to the surrounding bed and rim, and no text,
characters, HUD, logos, standing rings, or copied franchise imagery.

BM04 requested a bright tropical lagoon Battle arena with shallow turquoise
water as the readable central field, static sand, rock and vegetation around
the perimeter, and no text, characters, HUD, logos, standing rings, or copied
franchise imagery.

The generator did not author the layer contract or timeline. The production
builder derives masks and frame 01 deterministically from each source master.
