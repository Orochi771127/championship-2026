# Battle human-paint migration R4

This bounded batch applies the Owner-approved BM06-C mark-making direction to
the three structural edge cases that must pass before the remaining Battle
family can migrate.

- BM00 proves the shared layer can become quieter and more hand-painted without
  allowing generated pixels to move its canonical alpha geometry.
- BM07 proves the independent CYBERSPACE field can retain its identity without
  the common gold frame. Its six standing zones remain builder-owned.
- BM03 and BM04 prove the style can carry verified two-frame lava and water
  animation while retaining the existing composition and timing evidence.

The style direction is approved; the four migrated assets are still internal
review candidates. Nothing in this folder is runtime-eligible or shipping-ready.

## Locked visual language

- Broad value masses and visible brush overlap lead the image.
- The playable center stays quieter than foreground anchors and borders.
- Natural edges may break or disappear; gameplay geometry stays deliberate.
- Stone is matte and layered, metal is scuffed, and cyan glass is only
  selectively luminous.
- Small accents are rare and focal. Uniform chips, facets, chrome bevels and
  universal rim lights are rejected.

## Geometry and evidence guards

- BM00 output receives the previous canonical alpha channel exactly.
- BM07 rings are drawn at six fixed centers with radius 96 after image creation.
- BM03 remains 2 × 50 raw ticks; BM04 remains 2 × 20 raw ticks.
- BM04 retains its separately catalogued object layer.
- Animation remains visual-only; no gameplay meaning is inferred.
